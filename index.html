About Me

Hello! I am Professor Arockia Selvakumar, a passionate researcher and educator in the field of Robotics & Automation at the Department of Mechanical Engineering in Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) University, Chennai campus. In addition to my role as a professor, I also serve as the Placement Director, helping students secure career opportunities. My research has led to numerous publications in international journals, conferences, and book chapters, and I have been awarded multiple patents in robotics. I have also had the privilege of working as a visiting faculty in Germany, enriching my academic experience. Currently, I mentor 4 PhD students and have supervised 3 PhD graduates.
